Chops text into BPE sub-words. Sub-words ensure the model never encounters an out-of-vocabulary (OOV) word.

Library Search Analogy

Every token embedding is projected into 3 learned vectors using weight matrices $W_Q, W_K, W_V$:

Q (Query) = $x \cdot W_Q$ Search Term

"What am I looking for?"

K (Key) = $x \cdot W_K$ Book Tag

"What content do I contain?"

V (Value) = $x \cdot W_V$ Book Text

"What information do I pass on?"

The Core Formula:
$\text{Attention}(Q,K,V) = \text{Softmax}\left(\frac{Q \cdot K^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) \cdot V$

$\sqrt{d_k}$ scales dot products to avoid extreme gradients in softmax.

Interactive Attention Step-by-Step Calculator ($d_k = 4$)

Step 1: Raw Scores $(Q \cdot K^T)$ 3x3 tokens
Step 2: Scaled Scores $(/\sqrt{4} = 2)$ Div by 2
Step 3: Attention Weights (Softmax) Row sums = 1.0
Step 4: Output Vectors $(\text{Weights} \cdot V)$ Context Enriched
