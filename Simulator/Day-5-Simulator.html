1. Transformer Families Text Processing

Real-life Analogy:
• Encoder (Reader/Inspector): Reads an entire book simultaneously to summarize it.
• Encoder-Decoder (Translator): Reads a full sentence in English, processes context, then writes in Spanish.
• Decoder (Teleprompter Typist): Writes a story one word at a time, predicting the next best word.

2. Image Generation Patches & Latent Noise

Real-life Analogy:
• Patching: Cutting a photo into 16 puzzle pieces so the AI can treat visuals like visual "words".
• Diffusion: Like clearing fog off a window until a detailed picture emerges from static noise.

3. Behavior Adaptation Prompting vs LoRA vs Full Fine-Tune

Real-life Analogy:
• Prompting: Giving verbal rules before an exam.
• RAG: Letting the student use an open reference handbook.
• LoRA/QLoRA: Adding sticky notes to an existing textbook ($W + B \times A$).
• Full Fine-Tune: Rewriting the entire textbook from scratch (Expensive!).
